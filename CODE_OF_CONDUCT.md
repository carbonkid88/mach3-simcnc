# Code of Conduct

This project should be a practical, respectful place for people who are trying
to migrate CNC configurations safely.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep technical discussions specific and evidence-based.
- Share safety concerns clearly.
- Help new contributors anonymize data before they share it publicly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private machine, customer, or company data.
- Pressuring others to use unverified machine settings.
- Dismissing safety concerns around CNC motion, spindle control, limits, or I/O.

## Reporting Problems

If something in the project discussion becomes unsafe or inappropriate, open an
issue with a short description, or contact the repository owner through GitHub.
